Professional

I lead Customer Success for OEM Data Products at J.D. Power, and most of what I do is build the tools that make a team’s performance visible, and the stories behind it easy to tell.

Data only changes a decision when someone can follow it, and the analysts I work with make that happen. Our team delivers hundreds of high-quality analyses to OEM executives every year, and they power decisions that shape the whole industry. In order to retain and grow our analytical talent, I designed our analyst curriculum to teach Data, Analysis, Client Management and Communication skills.

I’ve built processes rooted in data collection, aggregation, and analysis that help our Data & Analytics, Customer Success team deliver better insights, add more value, and help our clients make better decisions. One is a live dashboard that flags at-risk accounts within days of a change in engagement.

Analytics projects on this page: Datamat and the Vehicle Performance Dashboard.
